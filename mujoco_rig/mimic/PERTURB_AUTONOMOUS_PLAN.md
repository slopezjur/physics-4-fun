## Counting and decision rules

A stage is a completed scientific question with its declared physical or
learning comparison, saved outcomes and interpretation. Implementation,
individual backend halves, checkpoint files, evidence repair and documentation
do not count as separate stages. A valid negative result completes a stage;
an admission failure does not establish a behavioral result. Never fabricate
completion to reach twenty.

Each stage receives a frozen protocol and budget before execution. Later
questions are selected from preceding findings. Do not schedule twenty
unconditional training runs. Use frozen diagnostics to resolve a concrete
uncertainty, then bounded matched learning comparisons when justified.
Preserve all sealed history, including failed admissions. Original active
target PD and zero passive springs remain the baseline. Every candidate
requires two-seed evidence and retained 120-second Stand before promotion.
